### **Project Overview**

The **Club de Algoritmia de la Universidad de Sevilla (CAUS)** is a student-led organization dedicated to fostering a collaborative environment for mastering competitive programming and algorithmic problem-solving. Founded by students from the Computer Engineering and Mathematics departments, it has grown into a cross-faculty community focused on technical excellence and professional preparation.

### **Key Contributions & Activities**
* **Technical Training:** organized weekly workshops focusing on **Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA)**, utilizing platforms like LeetCode and HackerRank to prepare members for high-level competitions and FAANG-style technical interviews.
* **Event Management:** co-organized the **CompliCAUS** internal coding contests and facilitated participation in prestigious tournaments such as the **Ada Byron University Programming Contest** and **Advent of Code**.
* **Industry Connection:** bridged the gap between academia and the professional world by hosting tech talks featuring industry experts, alumni, and researchers.
* **Community Building:** cultivated an inclusive culture through social initiatives (team-building activities and networking events), ensuring the club remains a space for both high-performance training and peer support.
